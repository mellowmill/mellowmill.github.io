v1.1.0

Pop-out windows & a friendlier workspace

This release is all about giving your notes room to breathe — pop the editor into its own window, work across two notes side by side, and shrink windows down to tuck a note into the corner of your screen.

  • Core Pop documents out into their own window.
    You can now pop the editor out into a separate window — handy for working across two notes side by side or spreading out over multiple monitors. New windows cascade so they don't stack on top of each other, and each has its own close button.
  • Free Core Friendlier workspace switching.
    The workspace chooser now shows readable, friendly names instead of raw folder paths, so it's easier to pick the right one at a glance.
  • Free Core Resize windows down much smaller.
    The minimum window size has been reduced to 200 × 300, so you can tuck a narrow note into the corner of your screen while you work.
  • Fix Pasting markdown tables with colons.
    Pasting markdown tables no longer breaks columns apart when a cell contains a colon (e.g. "Scenario: …"). Cells now split only on the proper : separator.
  • Fix Cleaner taskbar icon.
    The taskbar icon no longer shows a grey background plate behind it in the taskbar, Alt+Tab, and Task View.
  • Fix Fresher content on startup.
    Caches now refresh on startup and whenever you open a workspace, so you see up-to-date content sooner.
May 1, 2026
v1.0.0

Projects & Bring-Your-Own AI

Our first release introduces Projects to plan and track work in one place, plus an early vision of AI integration — connect your own AI agent and use it directly inside MellowMill Studio.

  • Core Introducing Projects.
    Define project scope, tasks, and due dates in one central place. Everything links back to the notes where the work actually lives, making it easy to track progress across notebooks without losing context.
  • Core Bring-Your-Own AI integration (first look).
    A first vision of AI inside MellowMill Studio — bring your own AI agent (BYO AI) and connect it to your workspace. Your notes stay local, you keep control of which provider and credentials are used, and there's no forced cloud or vendor lock-in.

Productivity for busy minds.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 17, 2026

MellowMill Studio ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect through our website and how we use it.

Website Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our website. Google Analytics collects:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring website or source
  • Browser type and device information
  • Approximate geographic location (country/city level)

This data is aggregated and anonymous. We do not use it to identify individual visitors. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

MellowMill Studio Application

MellowMill Studio is a local-first application. Your notes, tasks, and files are stored entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your content.

Cookies

Our website uses cookies only for Google Analytics. No advertising or tracking cookies are used.

Third-Party Services

We use Fourthwall for processing purchases. Their privacy policy governs any data collected during checkout.

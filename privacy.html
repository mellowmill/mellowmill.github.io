Privacy Policy

How MellowMill Studio handles your data.

Your Data Stays Local

MellowMill Studio is a local-first application. All your notes, tasks, and data are stored on your device in your Documents folder. We do not upload or sync your content to any server.

Third-Party AI & Connected Services

If you choose to connect third-party services such as AI providers, your data may be sent to those services according to their own terms and privacy policies. MellowMill Studio does not control how third-party services handle your data.

What We Collect

We collect minimal data to operate the product:

  • License validation: When you register or activate a license, your email address and order ID are sent to our server to verify your purchase.
  • Update checks: The app periodically checks for updates by contacting our update server. No personal data is sent during this process.

Your license and email data is stored on secure servers and is never sold or shared with third parties for marketing.

You may request to have your registration data deleted at any time by contacting us. Note that this may deactivate your access to specific features and functionality you have paid for.

What We Don't Collect

  • We do not collect analytics or usage telemetry.
  • We do not read or transmit your notes or files.
  • We do not use cookies or third-party trackers.

Third-Party Services

License purchases are processed by Fourthwall. Their privacy policy applies to transactions made through their platform.

Changes & Contact

We may update this policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes via the app or our website.

If you have questions, contact us at hello@mellowmill.studio.

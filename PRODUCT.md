## Platform

web

## Users

MellowMill serves busy professionals, sole traders, and small business owners who need to capture notes, decisions, tasks, and project context without switching between several tools. The documentation should support both people discovering MellowMill for the first time and existing users looking up a feature.

## Product Purpose

MellowMill is a privacy-first, local-first productivity workspace for bringing notes, tasks, projects, and deadlines together. It helps people capture the questions behind their work, record decisions, and turn those decisions into actions without losing context.

## Positioning

MellowMill works directly with local files in the Margin format, a structured plain-text format that captures meaning such as tasks, decisions, metadata, and links alongside readable notes. It is designed to keep data owned by the person using it rather than requiring a cloud workspace or recurring subscription.

## Operating Context

People use MellowMill as a focused workspace for professional notes, project planning, task tracking, decision logs, and reference material. Notes remain files on the user's machine and can be backed up or moved using the user's preferred file workflow.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Notes, tasks, tags, projects, deadlines, and links can live in one workspace.
- Margin is Markdown-compatible plain text and can be imported from or exported to Markdown.
- Full-text search works across notes, tags, and files.
- Images and files can be embedded or attached to notes.
- Free functionality includes unlimited notes, tasks, tags, local storage, lifetime updates, tables, interactive links, image and file support, Markdown import, and dark mode.
- Core functionality adds advanced tables, advanced tasks, advanced files, projects, AI integration, and 30-day version history.
- Bring-your-own AI connects through MCP. Claude Code is supported today; other providers and local models are planned.
- The application is currently available on Windows. Documentation should use platform-neutral language wherever the workflow allows and avoid promising unreleased platforms.
- AI features send relevant data to the chosen AI provider; local-first storage does not mean AI requests stay on the device.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is MellowMill. The voice is practical, calm, direct, and respectful of the reader's time. MellowMill values privacy, performance, fair one-time pricing, and simplicity.

## Product Principles

- Keep the work and its context together.
- Make ownership and portability the default.
- Reduce tool switching and avoid unnecessary ceremony.
- Let structure support real work without getting in the way.
- Make advanced capability additive rather than required.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What is Margin Language and how is it different from Markdown?

Margin is a markup language designed specifically for real work. Unlike Markdown, which focuses on formatting, Margin lets you capture structure — tasks, decisions, metadata, and links — all in one fluid syntax without switching tools. Find out more →

Is my data stored in the cloud?

No. MellowMill Studio is local-first by default. Your files live on your machine in plain text. You can back them up however you like — Git, Dropbox, a USB stick. Your data is yours to do with as you please. If you decide to bring your own AI features then your data will be sent to your AI provider.

Do I need to pay a subscription?

There is a totally free version of MellowMill Studio. There is also a Core version that has a one-time cost that gives more advanced features that professionals and small business owners will find more useful. We believe that your productivity tool shouldn't be a recurring bill.

What platforms does MellowMill Studio support?

MellowMill Studio is available on Windows, with MacOS, Linux, and mobile coming soon. Your notes are stored as simple files on your computer, so they're portable across any system.

Can I import notes from other apps?

Yes. MellowMill Studio supports importing from Markdown allowing you to bring in data from a wide variety of tools.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 17, 2026

Website Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first analytics service, to understand how visitors interact with our website. It collects aggregate, anonymous statistics such as:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring website or source
  • Browser type and device information
  • Approximate geographic location (country/city level)

Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies, local storage, or any other client-side state, and does not fingerprint or individually track visitors. The data collected is completely anonymous and cannot be used to identify you.

MellowMill is a local-first application. Your notes, tasks, and files are stored entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your content.

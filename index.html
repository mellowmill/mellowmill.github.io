Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before getting started.

What is Margin Language and how is it different from Markdown?

Margin is a markup language designed specifically for real work. Unlike Markdown, which focuses on formatting, Margin lets you capture structure — tasks, decisions, metadata, and links — all in one fluid syntax without switching tools. Find out more →

Is my data stored in the cloud?

No. MellowMill is local-first by default. Your notes, tasks, and files remain on your machine as plain-text files; MellowMill does not store them on its servers. Back them up with Git, Dropbox, a USB drive, or the workflow you already use. If you connect your own AI provider, the relevant data is sent to that provider to answer your request.

Do I need to pay a subscription?

Start with the free version, which includes unlimited notes, tasks, tags, and local storage. When your work needs projects, due dates, version history, advanced tables, or AI, upgrade to Core with a one-time payment. We believe your productivity tool shouldn't be a recurring bill, so all future Core upgrades are included for free.

What platforms does MellowMill support?

MellowMill is available on Windows, with MacOS, Linux, and mobile support planned. Your notes are stored as simple files on your computer, so they're portable across any system.

Can I import notes from other apps?

Yes. MellowMill supports importing from Markdown allowing you to bring in data from a wide variety of tools.

Privacy Policy

Effective date: April 17, 2026

MellowMill ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect through our website and how we use it.

Website Analytics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics, a privacy-first analytics service, to understand how visitors interact with our website. It collects aggregate, anonymous statistics such as:

  • Pages visited and time spent on each page
  • Referring website or source
  • Browser type and device information
  • Approximate geographic location (country/city level)

Cloudflare Web Analytics does not use cookies, local storage, or any other client-side state, and does not fingerprint or individually track visitors. The data collected is completely anonymous and cannot be used to identify you.

MellowMill Application

MellowMill is a local-first application. Your notes, tasks, and files are stored entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or have access to any of your content.

Cookies

Our website does not use cookies, trackers, or fingerprinting of any kind.
